# Damaged, Wrong or Defective Product Procedure

> SAMPLE DSports document, written for the demo knowledge base. It is a reference/demo procedure, not an official DSports
> policy or a manufacturer warranty.

## If a product arrives damaged

Keep the product and its packaging. Raise a support case and describe what is damaged. The assistant cannot look at photos,
so a support executive may ask you for them. A delivered order can also be returned within the return window (see the Return
Policy); the return eligibility check shows the exact deadline for your order.

## If you received the wrong product

Check the order to see what you bought, then raise a support case saying which item is wrong. A support executive compares
what you ordered with what you describe. A delivered order can be returned within the return window.

## If a product seems defective

Describe the fault in a support case. Whether a fault is covered depends on the warranty terms for the product (see the
Warranty information) and is decided by a person, not by the assistant. Product reviews are only context: they are not
proof that your product is faulty or not.

## If the product does not match its description

Say what you were told and what you received. A support executive compares the listing with the product. The assistant only
knows the product details that DSports has in its catalogue and will say when a detail is not available.

## If the product manual does not explain something

The assistant searches the product guides and care sheets in the knowledge base. If nothing there answers the question,
raise a support case so that a person can follow up.

## Safety

If a product injured you or could injure someone, stop using it and say so in the support case. A case flagged as
safety-related is shown to support as urgent. The assistant does not give medical advice.
