# DSports Refund Policy

> SAMPLE DSports document, written for the demo knowledge base. It describes how DSports refunds behave; it is not
> manufacturer documentation and not legal advice.

## What a refund is

A refund sends money back for a payment that was captured successfully. You can ask for a refund of the full payment or of
a smaller amount. The total you can have refunded can never be more than you paid, and amounts already requested count
against it.

## The refund lifecycle

Every refund has one of these statuses:

- **REQUESTED**: you asked for the refund and it is waiting for a DSports team member to review it. No money has moved.
- **PROCESSING**: the refund was approved and DSports is sending it through the payment provider. The status stays here
  while the provider's answer is still unknown, and the team can retry it.
- **COMPLETED**: the payment provider confirmed the refund. Only now does your payment show the refunded amount.
- **FAILED**: the payment provider declined the refund. The team can retry it.
- **REJECTED**: the team declined the refund request. A rejected refund does not count against the refundable amount.

## Payment status after a refund

When a refund completes for part of the amount, the payment shows **PARTIALLY_REFUNDED**. When the whole amount has been
refunded, the payment shows **REFUNDED**, and the order becomes **REFUNDED** too.

## When will the money arrive?

DSports shows you the status of the refund inside the system. How long your bank, card or UPI app takes to show the money
after a refund is **COMPLETED** depends on your payment provider, and DSports does not control or promise that time. The
assistant will never invent a date for it.

## Why has my refund not arrived?

Check the refund status first. If it is REQUESTED it has not been reviewed yet. If it is PROCESSING or FAILED it is
being handled by the team. If it is COMPLETED, DSports has finished its part and the remaining delay is with the payment
provider. If it is REJECTED, the request was declined.

## Refunds and returns

A return request (see the Return Policy) is separate from a refund. Returning a product does not automatically refund it.
