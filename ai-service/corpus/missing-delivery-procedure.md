# Missing or Late Delivery Procedure

> SAMPLE DSports document, written for the demo knowledge base. It is a reference/demo procedure, not an official DSports
> policy, and it contains no delivery-time or response-time commitment.

## Order has not arrived yet

Check the shipment status and the tracking events for your order (ask the assistant "where is my order"). A shipment moves
from CREATED, READY_FOR_PICKUP, PICKED_UP, IN_TRANSIT and OUT_FOR_DELIVERY to DELIVERED. If the shipment is moving and its
estimated delivery date has not passed, there is nothing to fix yet.

If the estimated delivery date has passed, or the shipment has had no tracking event for several days, or its status is
FAILED or RETURNED, raise a support case. A support executive reviews the shipment records for the order and decides the
next step.

## Order is marked delivered but you did not receive it

First check with the people at the delivery address and with neighbours, and look for a safe place where a parcel may have
been left. DSports shipment records show the status and the time of delivery. They do not include a signature, a photo or a
handover record, so the assistant cannot verify where a parcel was left.

If you still cannot find it, raise a support case. A support executive reviews the delivery records for your order and
decides what to do. What they decide, for example arranging a replacement or a refund through the normal DSports
processes, is theirs to decide; the assistant cannot promise an outcome.

## Only part of the order arrived

Shipment records show one shipment for the order and do not list which items were in the package. Tell support which item
is missing and raise a support case, so that a support executive can compare the order with what you received.

## What the assistant will and will not do

The assistant can read your order, shipment and payment records, explain what they show, tell you what it cannot verify,
and raise a support case for you after you confirm. It does not contact the courier or the warehouse itself, and it will
never say it did.
