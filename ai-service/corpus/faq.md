# DSports Frequently Asked Questions

> SAMPLE DSports document, written for the demo knowledge base. Not manufacturer documentation.

## Ordering and payment

### How do I place an order?
Add products to your cart, start checkout, choose or add a delivery address, pick a delivery method, check the summary and
place the order. Then pay for it; an order is only confirmed once the payment succeeds.

### How long do I have to pay?
An unpaid order is held for 30 minutes by default. After that it is cancelled automatically and the stock is released, so
you may need to start a new checkout.

### Can I cancel an order?
You can cancel an order that has not been paid yet. A paid order cannot be cancelled; after delivery you can use the return
and refund process instead.

### Which payment methods are available?
You choose the payment method at checkout. The methods you see there are the ones available to you.

## Tracking and delivery

### How do I track my order?
Open your orders, or ask the assistant "where is my order". You will see the shipment status, the tracking number that starts
with TRK, and the latest events.

## Returns and refunds

### How long do I have to return something?
7 days from delivery. See the Return Policy.

### Does a return refund me automatically?
No. A return and a refund are separate steps. See the Refund Policy for the refund statuses.

## Products

### Are product descriptions and sizes guaranteed?
Product details come from the DSports catalogue. If a detail is not listed for a product, DSports does not know it and the
assistant will say that it is not available rather than guess.

### Does the assistant know the current price and stock?
Yes, but it never remembers them: it asks the DSports shop for the live price and stock every time you ask.

## Warranty

### Do products have a warranty?
Cricket bats and footwear sold on DSports in this sample knowledge base have a limited warranty; the Warranty Card has the
exact terms.
