## The bat

The MRF Cricket Bat - Virgin (SKU CR-BAT-MRF-001) is an English willow bat of 1.2 kg for leather-ball cricket. It is a medium
weight bat intended as a first serious bat for beginners and club players.

## Knocking-in this bat

For this bat, plan for about 4 to 6 hours of knocking-in with a bat mallet, spread over 3 days, before facing a new leather
ball. Start with light taps on the face and increase the force gradually. Finish with a few sessions of gentle hitting against
an old ball in the nets.

## Oiling this bat

Apply two thin coats of raw linseed oil to the face and edges, 24 hours apart, and let each coat soak in with the bat lying flat.
Do not oil the handle or the splice.

## Care after each session

Wipe the blade dry, store it in its cover in a dry place, and keep it away from heat. Re-check the toe and edges every few weeks
for small cracks.
