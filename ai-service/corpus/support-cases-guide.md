# How Support Cases Work

> SAMPLE DSports document, written for the demo knowledge base. It describes how the DSports support-case feature behaves. It is
> not an official DSports service-level agreement: no response or resolution time is promised anywhere in it.

## Opening a case

You can ask the assistant to raise a case. It first checks your own records, tells you what it verified and what it could not,
and then asks you to confirm. Only after you confirm is the case created, and the case number (CASE- followed by a date and
a number) comes from the system. You cannot have two unresolved cases of the same kind about the same order: the assistant
shows you the existing one instead.

## Case statuses

- **OPEN**: received, and waiting for a support executive to start on it.
- **INVESTIGATING**: being looked at.
- **WAITING_FOR_CUSTOMER**: support asked you something; answer on the case and it moves back to INVESTIGATING.
- **WAITING_FOR_EXTERNAL_PARTY**: waiting for an answer from someone else, for example a courier or a payment provider.
- **RESOLVED**: a support executive recorded an outcome and a note.

## Asking for a person

Asking for a person to review a case records a **request**. It does not mean anyone has taken the case. The case shows when a
support executive has actually taken it. The assistant cannot connect you to a live agent.

## Messages and reopening

You can add a message to an unresolved case through the assistant. A resolved case can be reopened by you within the reopen
window (7 days after resolution by default); after that, raise a new case.

## What only staff can do

Only DSports staff can assign, prioritise, resolve or close a case, and only they see internal notes. The assistant cannot,
and refuses to try.
