# Support Triage SOP (internal)

> SAMPLE internal document for the POC. Staff only: never shown to customers. Not an official DSports procedure and it contains no SLA.

## Reading a case

Every case carries evidence in three groups. **BACKEND_VERIFIED** items were read by the backend from its own records when the case was
opened. **AI_INVESTIGATION** items were gathered by the assistant from the customer's own records and are not re-checked by the backend.
**CUSTOMER** items are the customer's statement. Verify an AI_INVESTIGATION item before relying on it for a financial decision.

## Priority

Priority comes from the documented rules: category, a confirmed duplicate payment, a high-value order, a repeat contact about the same
order, and an explicit safety flag. The reasons are on the case. Do not change priority because of the customer's tone or because they
contact support often.

## Working a case

Take the case (this also accepts an escalation request), set the queue if needed, add internal notes for colleagues and replies for the
customer. Resolve with an outcome and a note. Recording an outcome does not move money: refunds and return approvals use their own flows.
