# Warehouse and Regional Transfer Policy (SAMPLE internal document)

This is a SAMPLE policy written for the DSports AI proof of concept; it is not DSports' real logistics policy.

- Stock can be moved between regional warehouses to balance cover.
- Move stock out of a region only when it holds more than 60 days of cover.
- Move stock into a region that holds less than 21 days of cover, in quantities that restore about 30 days of cover.
- An inter-region transfer takes about 5 days and is raised by the warehouse operations team.
- The AI assistant suggests transfers from forecast demand and current regional stock; it never creates a transfer.
