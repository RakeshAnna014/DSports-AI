# Event and Tournament Preparedness Playbook (SAMPLE internal document)

This is a SAMPLE playbook written for the DSports AI proof of concept; it is not DSports' real planning document.

## Principles

- Demand during a major sporting event is not a fixed percentage. The uplift is estimated from past events of the same sport and event type, adjusted for the size of the event, and always shown with its range and its evidence.
- Where no history exists for a sport, the estimate is labelled a POC assumption and must not be treated as measured.

## Timeline

- Review readiness at least four weeks before the demand window starts.
- Orders that must arrive before the event should be placed at least one supplier lead time before the window starts.
- Categories with the strongest event effect are bats, jerseys, balls and gloves for cricket, and jerseys and boots for football. Kit bags and equipment are least affected.

## During and after the event

- Do not run clearance promotions on the event's sport during the demand window.
- After the event, review excess stock of event-driven categories within two weeks.
