# Clearance and Promotion Policy (SAMPLE internal document)

This is a SAMPLE policy written for the DSports AI proof of concept; it is not DSports' real pricing policy.

## When stock is reviewed for clearance

- Inventory older than 90 days is reviewed for clearance every month.
- Inventory with more than 120 days of cover is classed as slow moving; with at least 180 days of cover it is high overstock.
- Stock older than 120 days that also has at least 150 days of cover is critical aging and is reviewed first.

## Discount rules

- A clearance discount of up to 50% can be proposed by the merchandising team without further approval.
- A discount above 50% needs approval from the Head of Merchandising and the Finance controller.
- No promotion may leave a unit margin below 10% of the discounted selling price.
- Standard clearance promotions run for 14 days and are reviewed before they are extended.
- Do not start a clearance on a sport while one of its major events is in its demand window; stock is held for the event.

## Authority

- The DSports pricing and promotion engine is the only system that changes prices. The AI recommends a scenario; a business user must approve it and the pricing engine applies it, with an audit record.
- The AI assistant never changes a price and never launches a promotion.
