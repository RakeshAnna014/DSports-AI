# Category Strategy (SAMPLE internal document)

This is a SAMPLE document written for the DSports AI proof of concept; it is not DSports' real category strategy.

- Bats, balls and gloves (cricket) and boots (football) are core, fast-moving categories: protect availability and keep safety stock at the 95% service level.
- Jerseys are seasonal and event-driven: buy to the event forecast and clear leftovers soon after the season.
- Accessories have the most price-sensitive demand and respond strongly to promotions.
- Shoes respond least to discounts; prefer availability over discounting.
- High-value items such as bicycles are bought in small quantities (MOQ 2 to 6) and should not be overstocked.
